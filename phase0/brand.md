### What makes a good name here

- **Sayable over a phone call.** The business grows by word of mouth — a name that has to be spelt
  out loses referrals.
- **Spellable after hearing it once.** Clever spellings cost traffic forever.
- **Short.** It goes on a WhatsApp link that people share.
- **Not a generic noun-pair.** "SilkSareeShop" is unfindable, unownable, and untrademarkable.
