## The enquiry — send this verbatim

Send to **at least two** CAs. Do not engage the first one who replies.

> Subject: Enquiry — GST and structure advice for an online saree retail business
>
> I am starting an online saree retail business in India, selling direct to consumers across state
> lines via a website. Currently selling informally via WhatsApp with payments into a personal
> account. I need:
>
> 1. Advice on legal structure — sole proprietorship vs. private limited — for a business at
>    roughly `<<FILL: realistic annual turnover, e.g. "₹15-25 lakh in year one" | owner>>` turnover.
> 2. A written position on whether GST registration is required, given that sales will be
>    inter-state and online.
> 3. If GST is required: the applicable GST rate and HSN code for each of my product categories
>    (silk sarees, cotton sarees, blended/man-made fabric sarees), at the price points I sell at.
> 4. Confirmation of the invoice format required, including place-of-supply handling for
>    intra-state vs. inter-state sales.
> 5. What records I must keep and what returns must be filed, and at what frequency.
>
> Two questions before we begin:
> - Have you handled e-commerce or online sellers before?
> - What are your fees — for the initial advice, and for ongoing monthly/quarterly compliance?

**Why the two closing questions matter:** an accountant who has never handled an online seller will
get place-of-supply wrong, and place-of-supply drives whether every invoice carries CGST+SGST or
IGST. Getting it wrong is a correction exercise across every past order.
