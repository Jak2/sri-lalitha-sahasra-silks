# About — Draft Frame

> **Task 8, Step 1. This is a frame, not the text.** The story has to be in the owner's voice and
> contain facts only the owner knows. Anything written for them here would be marketing voice, which
> is the one thing this page must not be.

**PRD §8.1: a new saree site does not usually fail on technology. It fails because nobody believed
it.** The entire current business runs on relationships and word of mouth. This page is where a
referred stranger decides whether to trust a name they have never heard of. It is not filler — it
is weighted equally with checkout.

---

## The four questions the text must answer

200-400 words, in the owner's own words. Write it as if explaining to a customer in the shop.

1. **Who are you?** A name and a real person. Not "we are a team passionate about textiles."
2. **Where do the sarees come from?** Which weavers, which region, how they are chosen. This is the
   single most credible thing on the page, and the hardest to fake — which is exactly why it works.
3. **Why does this business exist?** The real reason. A family trade, a weaver relationship, a gap
   noticed. Buyers can tell the difference between a reason and a pitch.
4. **What does a buyer get here that a marketplace does not give them?** Curation, provenance, a
   person who answers the phone, one-of-a-kind pieces.

**Verify:** someone who does not know the business reads it and can say who they would be buying
from. If they cannot, the text has not done its job.

## Write it like this, not like that

| Avoid | Prefer |
|-------|--------|
| "Curated with love and passion" | "I pick every piece myself at the loom" |
| "Premium quality authentic handloom" | "Woven in `<place>` by `<weaver family>`" |
| "We strive to deliver excellence" | "If something is wrong, call me" |
| "Established with a vision to..." | "This started because..." |

Specific beats warm. A place name, a technique, a number of years — each of those is checkable, and
checkable is what trust is made of.

## Step 2 — Owner photograph

- ☐ A **real photograph of the owner** exists
- ☐ The owner consents to publishing it

**Stock photography here is worse than no photo at all.** A generic smiling model on an About page
is the clearest signal available that a site is not what it claims to be. If the owner does not want
their face published, use a real photo of the work — hands at a loom, the actual sarees, the actual
workspace — and say plainly why there is no portrait.

Store in `trust-assets/`. Sourcing photos — weavers, looms, the selection process — belong there
too, and carry real weight on this page.
