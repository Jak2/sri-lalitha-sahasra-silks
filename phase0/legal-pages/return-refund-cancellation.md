## Returns

**Window: 7 days from delivery.**

### We accept returns for

- The saree arrived **damaged in transit**
- The **wrong item** was sent
- The item is **significantly different** from its listing

### We cannot accept returns for

- **Minor colour variation between the photograph and the saree.** Colours render differently on
  every screen. We photograph honestly and do not edit colour beyond correcting for lighting.
- Sarees that have been **worn, washed, or altered**
- **Blouse pieces that have already been stitched or cut**
- Change of mind, where the item is as described

### What we need from you

**An unboxing video is required** for any damage or wrong-item claim. Start recording before the
parcel is opened and keep filming continuously through opening it. Without this we cannot raise a
claim with the courier, and we cannot verify the item left us in good condition.

This is not a formality to make claims hard — it is the only evidence that separates transit damage
from damage after delivery, and it is what lets us refund you quickly instead of investigating.

## Refunds

- Refunds are initiated **within 48 hours** of the returned item reaching us and passing a check.
- The refund goes back to the **original payment method**. We cannot redirect it elsewhere.
- Your bank or UPI provider then takes its own time to credit it — typically

## Exchanges

For a blouse-piece or fit issue we can offer an **exchange** instead of a refund, subject to
availability.

⚠️ Many of our sarees are **one-of-a-kind pieces** — where only one existed, an exchange for the
same saree is not possible. We will offer an alternative piece or a refund.

## One-of-a-kind pieces

A saree marked **"One-of-a-kind piece — only one available"** is exactly that. If two people order it
at the same moment, only one order can succeed; the other is **cancelled and refunded in full,
automatically**. You will hear from us directly when that happens.
